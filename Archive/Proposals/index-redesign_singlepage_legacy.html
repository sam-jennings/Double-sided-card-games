The Nine Signs

Every game reads the same nine signs differently — a Crown rules one game and builds the board in another. The pip is the rank; the picture is the story.

The same nine signs carry different truths in every game — that's the collection's premise, not a compromise.

Rules and concept sheets load live from each game's file. Player counts and play times are working estimates, finalised with each rulebook.
