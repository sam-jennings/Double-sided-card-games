# LODGE — Playtest Plan 01

*Pairs with the forthcoming `LODGE_playtest_01.md`. Written per `.kiro/steering/playtesting.md`. The hypotheses come from the game record's `next_step`, the rulebook's four open questions for the first table, and its 2/3/4P floor sim (design notes in [LODGE_rulebook.md](LODGE_rulebook.md)).*

## Open before the session

No rule gap blocks a 2P session. The v0.2 vetting closed the three ambiguities v0.1 had: one knock per round, the turned-over stock discard, and the face-seen-on-the-stock leak.

## Setup

- **Game:** LODGE
- **Version:** v0.2 (vetted; floor-simulated over 135,000 games at 2/3/4P; never played).
- **Players:** 2 — always testable, and the count the `next_step` names. Lodges 8 cards, stock 27 after the deal.
- **Symbol subset:** all 9 signs / 36 cards.
- **Session shape:** one **Still Water** teaching round (no Star power), then a **2-round base match** (one round per player, first seat swapping). If time allows, play a second base match with the first seat reversed.
- **Why this session:** LODGE is the collection's most familiar entry into the under-served deliberate-memory gap. The open questions (is the Turn fair, is a round too short, does the deck earn its place) are feel questions only a table can answer.

## Hypotheses to test

- **H1 — The Turn is a fair threat, not a cruel swing (the `next_step` question).** A Turn on a placed card can swing a lodge by up to 8 points. *Prediction:* when a Turn lands, the victim reacts with "I should have seen that coming" or a groan-laugh, not "that's unfair". By the second round, at least one player deliberately shows a lower face to avoid being a target. *Falsified if* a Turn draws a complaint of unfairness, or if neither player ever changes how they keep a card because of Turn risk.
- **H2 — A round is long enough to matter.** The sim puts skilled 2P rounds at **2.5 turns per player** (90th percentile 4.0) and random play at 8.8. *Prediction:* humans knock later than the bots, giving rounds of **4–7 turns each**, and nobody says "was that it?". *Falsified if* rounds regularly end within 3 turns each with a player feeling nothing happened. In that case, try the rulebook's recorded lever (knock only once your lodge shows no sign above Key) next session. Don't add cards.
- **H3 — The deck earns its place.** *Prediction:* at least once per round, a player reasons aloud from a rival's shown face or from the pond's registry about what is pressed. For example: "she placed that Crown herself, so it hides something low", or "the Crown–Moon card is in the pond, so that isn't under there". *Falsified if* nobody ever uses shown faces or pair counting and the game plays as plain Beverbende with extra steps. That would be a deck-necessity failure, the most serious result this session could produce.
- **H4 — Memory is a chosen challenge, not overhead.** *Prediction:* players find tracking their four pressed faces (and a swapped card's history) fun. Misremembering produces laughter rather than frustration. *Falsified if* players ask to re-peek, write things down, or say the memory load is a chore.
- **H5 — The 2P first-seat edge (58/42 in the sim).** Whoever reaches a light lodge first can knock with a turn in hand. *Prediction:* across the match, the first player wins more rounds than the second, and the rotating first seat makes the match feel even. *Falsified (in the good direction) if* humans knock late enough that seat order doesn't matter. *Flag if* the second seat feels doomed within a single round. One session can only spot a gross effect, so record it per round.
- **H6 — Powers are seasoning, not the meal.** The sim predicts about 0.9 powers per skilled 2P round (0.1 peeks, 0.4 swaps, 0.4 Turns). *Prediction:* 1–3 power uses per round. *Falsified if* powers dominate play (5 or more per round), or are never used.

## What to watch

Handling first:

- **The deal.** Cards must slide off the stock unturned. Watch for a card flipping while it is dealt, which would reveal a pressed face to everyone.
- **The first peek and Eye peeks.** Lifting the near edge with a shielding hand: watch for the pressed face flashing to the rival, and for a card coming down the wrong way up.
- **Swaps.** Cards slide across without lifting. Watch for accidental tilts and for players losing track of which slot moved.
- **The pond.** A replaced card is turned over onto the pond. Watch that both faces register with both players, and whether anyone uses the pond as a registry.
- **The stock top.** Its upper face is always public. Watch whether players notice that keeping a stock card with that face pressed gives the score away (the v0.2 FAQ point).
- **Reckoning.** Everyone turns every lodge card in place. Watch that the count is fast and undisputed.

Then:

- **Knock timing.** Record the turn and the knocker's estimate of their own total (ask after the reckoning).
- **Round length.** Time each round and the teach.
- **Talk.** Do players narrate deductions, bluff with their keeps, or play in silence?

## Observable success / failure signals

| | Holds (success) | Fails |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | Turns land as memorable swings; players adjust what they show | "That's unfair"; or Turn risk never changes a keep |
| H2 | 4–7 turns each; nobody feels short-changed | Rounds end in ≤ 3 turns each and feel empty |
| H3 | Shown-face or registry reasoning heard every round | Never heard; plays as plain Beverbende |
| H4 | Misremembering is funny; memory feels like the game | Requests to re-peek, notes, "it's a chore" |
| H5 | First-seat edge small or invisible over the match | Second seat feels doomed within a round |
| H6 | 1–3 powers per round | 5+ per round, or none |
| Handling | No flashed pressed faces; no wrong-way-up returns | Any of those repeatedly |

## Teaching plan

- One-sentence core: "Four cards in front of you; only the faces pressed to the table count against you; lowest total when someone knocks wins."
- Read the Nine Signs value table aloud and stress that **low is good**. The pip is the score.
- Demonstrate the deal slide, the near-edge peek and the swap slide once with spare cards before dealing for real.
- Play the **Still Water** round first (Peek and Swap only), then add the Star's Turn for the match. Show the rulebook's Ana–Ben Turn example with real cards.
- Say plainly that **only one player knocks**, and that pressing the face everyone saw on the stock makes that score public.
- Hold the strategy notes back until after the first base round (the rulebook labels them "read after your first game"). H3 tests whether shown-face reading is found unprompted.
- **Watch during teaching:** whether "only pressed faces score" needs repeating. Inverting the usual "what you see is what you have" is the likeliest snag.

## Out of scope

- **3P and 4P feel** (more targets for the Turn and Swap, and a longer pond registry). Carry them to a later session once 2P has answered H1–H3.
- **Dry Spell and Cold Knock** variants. Play them only after the base game works.
- **Re-simulation.** The floor is certified at all three counts; the table is the priority.
