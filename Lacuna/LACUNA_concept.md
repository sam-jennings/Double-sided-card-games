# LACUNA — the deck's missing tooth

*2–5 players · ~15 min · counting / deduction race · full 36 + one opaque sleeve · In development · physically unplayed*

> **Status & scope.** Wildcard batch, second flight (July 2026) — *originality first, rubric waived, unsimmed*. No rulebook, no sim, no rubric self-score; should not jump the [focus-priority.md](../.kiro/steering/focus-priority.md) queue on novelty alone. Portfolio index: [NEW_GAME_CONCEPTS.md](../NEW_GAME_CONCEPTS.md) §24.

**The trick that makes it exist.** On this deck the only thing that can be perfectly hidden is an **absence**. One card goes into an opaque sleeve (**the vault**) at the start; the mystery is not what's in players' hands but what's *missing from the world* — and because every pair exists exactly once, the pair-registry makes the absence provably identifiable. Negative-space deduction: the deck deduces itself, and the players race to finish the arithmetic first. (This revives the Parking Lot's "guess the missing card" — the UNPLAYED PAIR's good idea, freed from the trick-taking that killed it.)

**The one component.** An opaque card sleeve — policy tier 3, justified because it is small, generic, and already wanted elsewhere: JANUS's first table test improvised a deck-cover to stop top-card leaks; the sleeve *is* that fix, shared.

**Setup.** Shuffle; the top card's shown face is public (a backless deck insists) — slide it into the vault as it lies. So everyone knows the vaulted card shows sign V; the race is to name its **hidden partner** — one of eight candidates.

**Play.** The dealer reveals the remaining 35 one at a time: each card is turned once so **both faces flash publicly**, then placed into a growing tableau showing whichever face was up when drawn — the flashed face sinks back into hiddenness. The arithmetic: every sign lives on exactly 8 cards, so across all 35 reveals, every sign will be seen 8 times except two — V, and the vault's hidden partner, at 7. Track the tallies; when confident, **call**. A wrong call eliminates you from the round; a right call wins it and is verified by opening the vault (or by the count itself — the proof is public). Play rounds; rotate dealer.

**The texture.** Early calls are gambles on partial counts; late calls are certainties that probably lose the race. Elimination makes a rival's confident posture *information* — the counting race grows a poker face.

**Handling audit.** The memory load is the entire game — deliberate, symmetric, verifiable at the end (the flagged memory-gap genre, in its purest form). Nothing is consulted repeatedly; each flash is seen once by everyone, on equal terms.

**Honest risk (named).** This may be proctored mental arithmetic — nine running tallies at speed is a hard human task. The relief dial: track only *low* signs (dealer groups the tableau by shown sign, so shown counts are free and only hidden flashes need memory — that halves the load to a real but fair burden). If even that reads as homework, kill it cleanly.

**Fastest falsification.** Three rounds at 3P: if nobody ever calls before certainty (no race, just an audit) or every call is a coin-flip (no skill signal), the tension band doesn't exist.
