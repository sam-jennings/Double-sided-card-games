# MIRROR — show the face I'm thinking of

*2 players (or 4 as two rival pairs) · ~15 min · co-operative convergence / mind-meld · Stage 0 (concept)*

> **Status & scope.** Wildcard batch, second flight (July 2026) — *originality first, rubric waived, unsimmed*. No rulebook, no sim, no rubric self-score; should not jump the [focus-priority.md](../.kiro/steering/focus-priority.md) queue on novelty alone. Portfolio index: [NEW_GAME_CONCEPTS.md](../NEW_GAME_CONCEPTS.md) §27.

**The trick that makes it exist.** The almanac's four seasons are structurally identical 9-card hands — and each season shows **every sign exactly twice** across its eighteen faces. So two players can hold provably isomorphic hands, and — because which season each holds is public, and played cards are visible — each can *compute the other's exact remaining options* at every moment. That is the machinery for a mind-meld game (Medium, Wavelength) with a spine those games lack: the psychic guess is constrained by an arithmetic both players share. Empathy, made computable at the edges.

**Setup.** Sort the deck by the almanac; each player takes one season; the other two seasons shuffle into a **prompt deck** of 18.

**A round.** Flip a prompt: it shows sign S. Both players secretly choose one card and one face from their hand, trying to show the **same sign as each other** — and showing S itself is forbidden (the focal point is removed; you must converge on an *association*: prompt Flame — do you both reach for Wave, which quenches it, or Star, which it throws?). Palms-commit, simultaneous reveal (the COUNCIL protocol). Match: both cards bank, one point. Miss: both cards burn. Nine rounds; hands empty either way.

**The arc (the design's heart).** Early, hands are rich: every sign showable two ways, and the game is pure association — a couple's private mythology forming in real time. Late, hands are ragged: the showable sets shrink, *and both players can derive both sets exactly* (complement deduction on a public season). The final rounds invert the game — less "what does Flame mean to us?" and more "what is the only sign we can both still show?" — psychology annealing into logic over nine rounds, automatically, with no rule saying so.

**Scoring & rivalry.** Solo pair: score out of 9, chase your record (the couples' game the collection lacks). Four players: two pairs alternate prompts off the same deck; the burn pile punishes noise; first pair to 5 matches.

**Handling audit.** Simultaneous secret choice from held fans is established (THE COUNCIL); everything else is open. No memory: the entire deduction layer is re-derivable from the visible banked/burnt rows. Zero components.

**Fastest falsification.** Nine rounds with a partner: if the misses feel like the *deck's* fault rather than "we weren't thinking the same thing yet", the association layer isn't carrying — the tell is whether a miss starts a conversation or ends one.
