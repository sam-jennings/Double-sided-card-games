# DOWRY — Playtest 01

*Retrospective record · physical 2-player playtest*

The 2-player base game was physically playtested and worked extremely well. The designer's assessment is that DOWRY may be the best game in the collection so far.

## Verdict

**Proven.** The core cut-and-choose bluffing game works and has no known blocking problem.

## Evidence retained

- The physical flow worked at the table.
- The overall experience was exceptionally strong.
- No blocking rules or handling problem was reported.

The original detailed session notes were not present in the repository when this record was written, so no finer observations are inferred here.

Confirm the result with another 2-player match, then test the separate 3–4-player variants before making any claims about those modes.
